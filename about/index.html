Hi, I’m Louis.

I’m a cabinet designer in Malaysia, focused on personal spaces that balance useful storage with a clean, considered aesthetic.

This portfolio brings my personal design approach together in one place. My role is to understand your routines, make sense of your storage needs and translate your ideas into a clear cabinetry plan.

Whether it’s a kitchen, wardrobe or living room, we work through the space, materials and budget together, keeping the design grounded in everyday life.
